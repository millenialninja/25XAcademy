Our websites use:

- Some live sessions are recorded. We say so at the start of the session. You can keep your camera and mic off and use a display name.
- **The ₹99 workshop recordings are not shared with attendees.** We may use them internally to improve the session.

## 9. Keeping your data safe

We use reasonable security measures. That means access only for people who need it, strong passwords and two-factor login on the tools that hold your data, and trusted vendors with their own security programs. No system is perfectly secure.

If a personal data breach affects you, we'll tell you without delay: what happened, what it means for you, and what we're doing about it. We'll also report it to the Data Protection Board of India and to CERT-In, as the law requires.

## 10. Data stored outside India

Some of our vendors (for example Meta, Zoom and website hosts) store data outside India. We transfer data only as the DPDP Act allows, and never to a country the Government of India has restricted.

## 11. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you can:

- **Ask what data we hold about you** and who we've shared it with.
- **Correct, complete or update it.**
- **Ask us to delete it**, unless the law requires us to keep it (for example, invoices).
- **Withdraw your consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it. This doesn't affect what we did before, and we may not be able to continue a service that needs that data.
- **Nominate someone** to use these rights for you in case of death or incapacity.
- **Complain to us**, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if you're not satisfied.

To use any of these rights, email hi@25xacademy.com from the address you used with us, or write to our Grievance Officer (section 13). We'll reply within **30 days**.
