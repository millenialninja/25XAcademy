## 2. Why most programs aren't refundable after they start

Our programs are live and digital. Once you've joined the sessions, received the materials and prompts, or entered the community, we can't take that back. So we don't offer refunds for:

- changing your mind after the refund window,
- missing live sessions for personal reasons, or not using the materials,
- not getting a particular result (see "No guaranteed results" in our Terms),
- not having a suitable laptop, internet connection or software,
- a price being different before or after you bought.

If you can't attend a ₹99 workshop batch, write to us **before Day 1** and we'll try to move you to a later batch. This depends on availability.

## 3. When you always get a full refund

Whatever the program, we refund in full if:

1. **You were charged twice** for the same program, or **charged the wrong amount**.
2. **We cancel a program** and you don't want, or can't attend, the new date or alternative we offer.
3. **We fail to give you access** to what you paid for, and don't fix it within 7 days of you telling us.
4. **What we deliver is materially different from what the program page promised**, and we can't put it right within a reasonable time.
5. **The law, a Consumer Commission or another authority** requires a refund.

There is no short deadline for these. Please tell us as soon as you notice.

## 4. How to ask for a refund

Email **hi@25xacademy.com** from the email address you bought with, and include:

- your name and phone number,
- the program name and date of purchase,
- the payment or order ID (from your confirmation email or invoice),
- the reason, with screenshots if it's a payment or access problem.

If you're on an instalment plan and your refund is approved, we refund all instalments you've paid and cancel the rest. If you stop using a program outside the refund window, the remaining instalments are still due, as agreed at checkout.
