## The problem with most business websites

Most business websites are brochures. They list services, show a few photos and end with "Contact us". Visitors read, nod and leave, and you never learn who was interested.

Only a small share of visitors ever fill in a contact form. The rest might have been good customers, but a form asking them to "get in touch" gives them no reason to act today.

## What a business tool does differently

A business tool gives the visitor something **useful and personal, right now**:

- A CA firm's **GST readiness check** tells a business owner where their filing gaps are.
- An interior designer's **budget estimator** shows a homeowner what their project might cost.
- A coach's **readiness assessment** tells someone whether coaching is right for them yet.

To see their full result, the visitor shares their name, WhatsApp number and need. They're happy to, because they get something in return. You get a lead that already tells you what they care about.

## Side by side

| | Website | Business tool |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Tells people about you | Does something for them |
| Visitor's effort | Read and decide whether to contact you | Answer a few questions, get a result |
| What you learn | Usually nothing, unless they fill a form | Name, WhatsApp number, need, and their answers |
| First conversation | "Tell me what you need" | "I saw your score. Let's talk about your weakest area." |
| Works for | Trust, information, search | Turning interest into leads |

## Do you need both?

Usually, yes. Your website is where people check that you're credible. Your tool is what you **share**: in WhatsApp messages, on LinkedIn, in your Instagram bio, on a QR code at events, and as the main button on your website.

## Which tool should you build first?

Start from the one question customers always ask before they buy:

- "How much will it cost?" → build an **estimator** or **quote calculator**.
- "Am I ready for this?" → build a **readiness check**.
- "Which option is right for me?" → build a **finder** or **recommendation quiz**.
- "How am I doing compared to others?" → build a **scorecard**.
